About Talgunda Startups

We're a forward-thinking startup ecosystem dedicated to transforming ideas into successful ventures through innovation, strategic guidance, product development, training and collaborations.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, Talgunda Startups was established to support innovators and entrepreneurs in transforming ideas into viable, market-ready businesses.

We have grown into a comprehensive startup support ecosystem, providing product development, training, mentorship, and industry connections.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that shape our approach and define our purpose

Mission

Our mission is to transform waste and raw materials into marketable novel products and empowering individuals through education, skill development, and sustainable entrepreneurship.

Identify the gap and fill it by transforming it into wealth.
Foster a collaborative industrial and entrepreneurial community.
Bridge the gap between innovation and market sustainability.

Vision

We care nature.

Promote harmony between nature, industry, and human development.
Promote industry-driven innovation and entrepreneurship

Mission in Action

We actively work towards this mission through our programs, training, transforming, building networks, and hands-on mentorship initiatives.
